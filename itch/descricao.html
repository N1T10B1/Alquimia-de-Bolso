Como se joga

[[fig-passos]]

Ouça o cliente. Ninguém pede "um Elixir de Cura". Pedem coisas como "cortei a mão descascando batatas para cem convidados" ou "quero um trovão de bolso para acordar meu irmão, que dorme demais". As pistas estão no que eles contam.

Misture no caldeirão. Escolha duas cartas de ingrediente e veja o que sai. Acertou, nasce uma poção e o cliente sai feliz. Errou, sai Lodo Instável, que ao menos fica anotado para você não repetir.

Escreva o caderno. Cada descoberta vira uma página. Venda, junte moedas, compre utensílios melhores e não esqueça: o aluguel do Seu Leopoldo vence todo fim de dia.

Gota Lunar, Brasa Rubra, Folha Viva, Pó de Cristal, Sombra Engarrafada, Orvalho Solar, Cogumelo Azul e Pena do Vento. Cada um tem uma qualidade (úmido, quente, vivo, duro, escuro, brilhante, terroso, leve), e o herbário que a Mestra deixou no fim do caderno conta o que cada um faz. O resto é com você.

English

Your master knew every recipe by heart. The book she left you is blank.

Mistress Benvinda has gone to see the sea and left you Alquimia de Bolso ("Pocket Alchemy"), the narrowest shop in the village of Crooked Hill (Ladeira Torta), squeezed between the bakery and the wall. With it came a copper cauldron, a few flasks and a recipe book... with every single page blank. She never wrote anything down: she knew it all by heart.

Now the customers are lining up at the counter, and every recipe is yours to discover.

[[mistura]]

How to play

[[fig-passos]]

Listen to the customer. Nobody asks for "a Healing Elixir". They say things like "I cut my hand peeling potatoes for a hundred guests" or "I want a pocket thunderclap to wake up my brother, who sleeps way too much". The clues are in what they tell you.

Mix in the cauldron. Pick two ingredient cards and see what comes out. Get it right and a potion is born. Get it wrong and you brew Unstable Sludge, which at least goes in the book so you never make it twice.

Fill the book. Every discovery becomes a page. Sell, save coins, buy better tools and don't forget: Mr. Leopoldo's rent is due at the end of every day.

Eight ingredients, twelve recipes

[[fig-ingredientes]]

Moon Drop, Ruby Ember, Living Leaf, Crystal Dust, Bottled Shadow, Sun Dew, Blue Mushroom and Wind Feather. Each one has a quality (wet, hot, alive, hard, dark, bright, earthy, light), and the herbarium your master left at the back of the book tells you what each one does. The rest is up to you.

[[fig-pocoes]]

Who comes to the counter

[[fig-clientes]]

Granny Quitéria, who has all the time in the world. Sir Tadeu the Knight, always on an urgent quest. Lumi the Fairy, who can't keep still. Baltazar the Dwarf, stubborn but patient. Cirilo the Bard, who has a show in a few minutes. Anselmo the Elf, who lives for centuries. Dorotéia the Pirate, because the tide waits for no one. Bartolomeu the Talking Cat, who knocks your flasks over to get attention. And Ofélia the Witch, who waits as long as a spell takes. At night, Zuleica the Merchant rolls by with her cart full of ingredients.

In this demo

Play it

  • In the browser: click "Run game" above. Works on desktop and mobile.
  • Windows: download the .zip, extract it and open Alquimia de Bolso.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway": it only happens because the game is new and independent.
  • Android: download the .apk and tap it to install. Your phone will ask you to allow apps from this source.
  • Controls: mouse or touch. Swipe down to open the book.
  • Languages: English and Brazilian Portuguese (switch in the menu).
